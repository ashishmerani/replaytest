# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately, using GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository (open the repository's Security tab and choose Report a vulnerability). Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. We do not use an email address for security reports.

We will acknowledge your report and keep you informed as the investigation progresses. A confirmed fix ships in a release.

## Scope

replaytest runs on your machine and reads from your own PostHog project. Reports are most useful when they explain how the tool could expose your captured session data, your credentials, your local files, or access to your PostHog project.
