# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in replaytest! We're not accepting pull requests yet, but we'd love to hear from you.

**Open an issue** to tell us what you'd like to see, what isn't working, or how you're using replaytest. Every issue is read, and they directly shape what we build next. Questions are welcome too.

To see what's planned, check the [roadmap](https://github.com/users/ashishmerani/projects/3).

To report a security problem, please follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.
